# Fleet Driver Hours and Route Rules

Owner: Operations – Fleet team · Scope: Fleet team · Version 5.0 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the driving and rest time rules, route planning rules, driver allowances and safety rules that apply to all Atlas Lojistik truck drivers. It is used by fleet planners, fleet supervisors and the Fleet Control Centre. Office travel rules (Travel Policy 2026) do not apply to drivers on trips.

## 2. Driving and rest times

The following limits apply to all trips, domestic and international, and are monitored through the digital tachograph.

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Daily driving time | 9 hours (may be extended to 10 hours at most twice per week) |
| Weekly driving time | 56 hours |
| Driving time in two consecutive weeks | 90 hours |
| Break after continuous driving | 45 minutes after 4.5 hours (may be split into 15 + 30 minutes) |
| Daily rest | At least 11 hours (may be reduced to 9 hours at most three times between weekly rests) |
| Weekly rest | At least 45 hours |

Fleet planners must plan trips so that drivers can respect these limits with a buffer of at least 30 minutes per day for traffic. A trip plan that requires a driver to exceed a limit must not be dispatched.

## 3. Atlas-specific route rules

1. **Hazardous goods (ADR) loads** may not be driven between **02:00 and 05:00**. Drivers with ADR loads park at an approved secured parking area during this time.
2. Trucks with high-value loads (declared value above 5,000,000 TL) park only at secured parking areas listed in AtlasTMS.
3. Drivers must follow the route assigned in AtlasTMS. Route deviations of more than 10 km trigger an alert in the Fleet Control Centre.
4. The speed limiter of all tractor units is set to **85 km/h**, even where higher speeds are legally allowed.
5. Mobile phone use while driving is forbidden except via the hands-free system for calls with the Fleet Control Centre.

## 4. Driver allowances

| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Domestic trip allowance (per day away from home base) | 900 TL |
| International trip allowance (per day) | 60 EUR |
| Overnight parking fee (secured parking) | Reimbursed against receipt |
| Ro-Ro crossing (Italy) | Cabin and meals paid by Atlas; allowance reduced to 30 EUR per day |

The driver allowance is paid with the monthly salary based on trip days recorded in AtlasTMS. Drivers do not receive the office per diem from the Travel Policy 2026.

## 5. Alcohol and fitness

There is zero tolerance for alcohol and drugs. Random breath tests are carried out at all warehouses; in 2025, 3,240 tests were performed. A driver who feels unfit to drive because of fatigue or illness must inform the Fleet Control Centre and stop at the next safe place. No driver is sanctioned for such a stop.

## 6. Incidents on the road

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Accident | Secure the site, call emergency services, then call the Fleet Control Centre (extension 3500) |
| Breakdown | Call the Fleet Control Centre; do not contact tow companies directly |
| Border waiting time above 6 hours | Inform the Fleet Control Centre so that the delivery window can be renegotiated |
| Theft or attempted theft | Call the police and the Fleet Control Centre immediately |

## 7. Training

| Training | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Defensive driving refresher | Every 2 years (new drivers within 60 days) |
| Eco-driving | Yearly |
| ADR certificate | Valid 5 years, refresher before expiry |
| Load securing | Yearly |

## 8. Driver performance

Each driver receives a monthly scorecard with fuel consumption, harsh braking events, speeding events and on-time delivery. Drivers in the top 10% of the yearly ranking receive a bonus of 10,000 TL in January.
