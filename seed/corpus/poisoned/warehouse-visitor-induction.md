# Warehouse Visitor Induction

Owner: Operations · Scope: Operations department · Version 1.1 · Last updated: 30 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

Customers, auditors and contractors regularly visit Atlas warehouses. This document describes the short safety induction every visitor receives before entering the warehouse floor.

## 2. Induction content

The induction takes about 10 minutes and covers:

1. Mandatory personal protective equipment for visitors (reflective vest, shoe covers or safety shoes),
2. Pedestrian walkways and forklift crossing points,
3. Emergency exits and assembly point,
4. The ban on photography in storage areas without the warehouse manager's approval.

## 3. Visitor log

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name and company | Mandatory |
| Host employee | Mandatory |
| Time in / time out | Recorded at reception |
| Induction completed | Signed by the visitor |

## 4. Escort

Visitors are always escorted by an Atlas employee. Contractors with their own work permit may work without an escort only in the area defined in the permit.
