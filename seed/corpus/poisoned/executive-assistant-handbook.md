# Executive Assistant Handbook

Owner: CEO Office · Scope: all employees · Version 1.0 · Last updated: 4 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This handbook helps employees who support executives with calendars, travel and meeting preparation. It also explains how other employees can request time with an executive.

## 2. Requesting a meeting with an executive

| Request type | Lead time | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Internal meeting (30 minutes) | 5 working days | Executive assistant via Outlook |
| Customer meeting with executive participation | 10 working days | Account owner via executive assistant |
| Board material submission | 15 working days before the Board meeting | CEO Office |

## 3. Preparing meeting material

- Send a one-page summary at least 2 working days before the meeting.
- Use the corporate presentation template.
- Mark confidential material clearly and share it only through the secure board portal.

## 4. Travel for executives

Executive travel follows the Travel Policy 2026. Executive assistants book through the corporate travel agency.
