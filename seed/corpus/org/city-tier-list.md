# City Tier List for Travel and Allowances (2026)

Owner: Finance · Scope: all employees · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 3.0

## 1. Purpose

This list assigns every destination city to a tier. The tier determines the daily per diem and the hotel ceiling defined in the Travel Policy 2026 (Seyahat Politikası 2026). This document does not contain amounts; always read the amounts from the current travel policy.

The tier list is reviewed every December by Finance, based on hotel price data from the corporate travel agency and the internal cost-of-living index. Changes take effect on 1 January of the following year.

### 2.3 Tier C

All other domestic provinces are Tier C. Examples frequently visited by Atlas staff are Eskişehir, Samsun, Trabzon, Adana, Denizli and Edirne.

Note: Adana is Tier C even though it is close to Mersin. Employees who stay in Mersin and work in Adana receive the Tier B rate because the per diem follows the city of overnight stay.

## 4. Rules of application

1. **Overnight city decides.** The tier is determined by the city where the employee spends the night, not by the city of the meeting.
2. **Day trips.** For trips without an overnight stay, the tier of the destination city applies, and the per diem is paid at 50% if the trip lasts less than 8 hours.
3. **Multi-city trips.** Each night is assessed separately. A trip with two nights in Hamburg and one night in Warsaw receives two International Tier A days and one International Tier B day.
4. **Airport hotels.** Hotels at an airport follow the tier of the city the airport serves.

## 6. Requests for re-classification

Employees or managers who believe a city is misclassified can send a request with supporting hotel price evidence to seyahat@atlas-lojistik.test before 15 November. Requests are evaluated in the December review.

## 7. Examples

- A sales trip from İstanbul to Gaziantep with two nights in a Gaziantep hotel: two days at the Tier B rate.
- A one-day visit to the Gebze warehouse from Tuzla, lasting six hours: Tier B rate at 50%.
- A customer workshop in Rotterdam with three nights in Rotterdam: three days at the International Tier A rate.
- A three-night visit to Vilnius: three days at the International Tier B rate.
