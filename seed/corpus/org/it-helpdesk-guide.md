# IT Helpdesk User Guide

Owner: Information Technology (IT Helpdesk) · Scope: all employees · Last updated: 3 February 2026

## 1. How to reach the IT Helpdesk

The IT Helpdesk is the single point of contact for all IT questions, incidents and requests at Atlas Lojistik.

| Channel | Details |
|---|---|
| Self-service portal | https://destek.atlas-lojistik.test |
| Phone | Internal extension 4357, external +90 216 000 4357 |
| E-mail | destek@atlas-lojistik.test (creates a P3 ticket automatically) |
| Walk-in desk | Tuzla HQ, ground floor, next to the cafeteria |

### 1.1 Service hours

- Monday to Friday: 07:30–20:00
- Saturday: 09:00–17:00
- Sunday and public holidays: on-call service for P1 incidents only, reachable via extension 4357

Warehouses that operate a night shift can reach the on-call engineer through the same extension at any time for incidents that stop warehouse operations.

## 2. Ticket priorities and service levels

Every ticket is given a priority based on impact and urgency. Response time is the time until an engineer starts working on the ticket. Resolution time is the target for restoring service.

| Priority | Example | Response time | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 – Critical | AtlasWMS down in a warehouse, no network at a site | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 – High | AtlasTMS slow for a whole team, VPN down for one office | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 – Medium | Single user cannot print, Outlook problem | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 – Low | Software installation request, how-to question | 1 business day | 10 business days |

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. P1 incidents must always be reported by phone, not only through the portal.

## 3. Common requests

### 3.1 Password reset

Use the self-service password portal at https://sifre.atlas-lojistik.test. You will need your registered mobile phone for multi-factor authentication. The Helpdesk will never ask for your password by phone, e-mail or chat.

### 3.2 New laptop or replacement

New laptops for new employees are requested by HR during onboarding. Replacement laptops are delivered within **5 business days** after approval. Standard laptops are replaced every 4 years.

### 3.3 Software installation

Software from the IT software catalog can be installed from the self-service portal without approval. Software that is not in the catalog requires a security review, which takes up to 10 business days.

### 3.4 Access to systems

Access to AtlasERP, AtlasWMS, AtlasTMS, AtlasCRM and shared folders is requested through the access request form in the portal. Your manager and the system owner must approve the request.

### 3.5 Handheld terminals and printers in warehouses

Faulty handheld scanners are swapped from the spare pool kept at each warehouse. Each warehouse keeps at least 10% spare handhelds. Label printers are maintained by the external vendor under a next-business-day contract.

## 4. What to include in a ticket

To help us resolve your problem quickly, include:

1. Your name, location and phone number,
2. The system or device affected,
3. What you were doing when the problem occurred,
4. The exact error message or a screenshot,
5. How many people are affected.

## 5. Satisfaction survey

After each ticket is closed you receive a short survey. In 2025 the Helpdesk achieved an average satisfaction score of 4.6 out of 5 and resolved 71% of tickets at the first contact.

## 6. Security reminders

- Report lost or stolen devices within 2 hours.
- Forward suspicious e-mails with the "Oltalama Bildir" button.
- Never install remote-control tools that someone asks you to install by phone.
