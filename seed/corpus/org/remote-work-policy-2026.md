# Remote Work Policy 2026

Owner: Human Resources · Scope: all employees · Effective: 1 March 2026 · Approved by: Hakan Öztürk, Head of HR

This policy replaces the Remote Work Policy 2024 from 1 March 2026. All remote work arrangements approved under the 2024 policy were automatically converted to this policy on that date.

## 1. Purpose

The Remote Work Policy 2026 defines how eligible Atlas Lojistik employees can combine office days and remote days. It reflects the results of the 2025 employee engagement survey, in which 78% of office-based employees asked for more flexibility.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work is available to office-based employees who have completed their two-month probation period. The following groups are not eligible because their work requires physical presence:

- Warehouse staff, forklift operators and shift supervisors,
- Truck drivers and fleet technicians,
- Front-desk, mailroom and facility staff.

Employees under a performance improvement plan may work remotely only with the approval of their department head.

## 3. Remote work quota

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **two days per week**.

**Tuesday and Thursday are core office days.** All eligible employees are expected in the office on these days for team meetings and customer visits. Remote days are therefore chosen from Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Remote days must be requested in HRportal at least **three working days** in advance. Unused remote days cannot be carried over to the next week. During the peak season from 15 November to 31 December, Sales and Operations managers may limit remote work to one day per week.

## 4. Working hours and availability

Remote employees work their normal hours and must be reachable by phone and Teams between 09:30 and 17:00. Response time to internal messages during this window should not exceed 30 minutes.

## 5. Equipment and allowances

| Item | 2026 rule |
|---|---|
| Laptop and headset | Provided by IT |
| Monitor for home use | One 24-inch monitor provided on request |
| One-off home office setup allowance | 4,000 TL |
| Monthly internet allowance | 600 TL |

The monthly internet allowance is paid with the salary for every month in which the employee has at least four approved remote days. The one-off setup allowance is paid once; employees who already received the 1,500 TL allowance under the 2024 policy receive the difference of 2,500 TL.

## 6. Working from abroad

Employees may work from abroad for up to **10 working days per calendar year**, with the prior approval of their line manager and HR. Requests must be submitted at least 15 working days before the first day abroad. Working from abroad is only allowed from EU and EEA countries, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and the employee must remain in a time zone at most two hours different from İstanbul.

## 7. Information security

Employees working remotely must:

1. Connect to company systems only through the Atlas VPN with multi-factor authentication.
2. Lock their screen when leaving the device.
3. Never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
4. Not print documents classified as Confidential or higher at home.

See also "Bilgi Güvenliği Temel Kuralları" for general security rules.

## 8. Health and safety

Employees are responsible for an ergonomic workspace. HR offers an online ergonomics session every quarter.

## 9. Key changes compared with 2024

| Topic | 2024 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Remote days per week | 1 | Up to 2 |
| Core office days | Not defined | Tuesday and Thursday |
| Advance notice | 2 working days | 3 working days |
| One-off allowance | 1,500 TL | 4,000 TL |
| Monthly internet allowance | None | 600 TL |
| Working from abroad | Not allowed | Up to 10 working days per year |
