# HR Onboarding Operations Handbook

Owner: Human Resources · Scope: HR department · Version 3.2 · Last updated: 10 March 2026

## 1. Purpose

This handbook describes the steps HR takes to onboard a new employee at Atlas Lojistik A.Ş., from signed offer to the end of probation. It is written for HR staff; hiring managers receive a shorter checklist generated from HRportal.

## 2. Timeline overview

| When | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Offer signed | Create pre-hire record in HRportal | HR Operations |
| 10 business days before start | Request documents from the candidate | HR Operations |
| 7 business days before start | Submit laptop and access request to IT | HR Operations |
| 5 business days before start | Assign a buddy and confirm desk or locker | Hiring manager |
| 1 business day before start | Social security (SGK) entry completed | HR Operations |
| Day 1 | Welcome session, ID badge, safety briefing | HR and site manager |
| Day 30 | First onboarding survey | HR |
| End of month 2 | Probation decision | Hiring manager and HR |
| Day 90 | Second onboarding survey and buddy programme closure | HR |

## 3. Before the first day

### 3.1 Required documents

HR collects the following documents through the HRportal candidate area:

1. Copy of national ID card,
2. Diploma or graduation certificate,
3. Residence certificate (e-Devlet printout),
4. Criminal record certificate not older than 3 months,
5. Health report for warehouse and fleet roles,
6. Driving licence and SRC/psychotechnical certificates for drivers,
7. Bank account information for salary payment.

Documents are stored in the personnel file and are classified as Confidential.

### 3.2 IT request

The laptop and access request must reach IT at least **7 business days** before the start date. Requests submitted later are processed, but HR must inform the hiring manager that the laptop may not be ready on day 1. Warehouse employees receive a handheld terminal account instead of a laptop.

### 3.3 Social security entry

The SGK entry must be completed **no later than one business day before the start date**. No employee may start work, including orientation, before the SGK entry is confirmed.

## 4. The first day

The first day follows a fixed agenda at Tuzla HQ; warehouse and fleet employees follow the site version.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Welcome and coffee with HR |
| 09:30 | Company overview video and values |
| 10:30 | ID badge photo, locker and shuttle registration |
| 11:00 | Occupational health and safety briefing (mandatory, 2 hours for warehouse and fleet) |
| 13:00 | Lunch with the buddy |
| 14:00 | IT setup: laptop, MFA, password portal |
| 15:00 | Meeting with the hiring manager |

## 5. Buddy programme

Every new employee gets a buddy from the same department, but not from the same reporting line. The buddy meets the new employee at least once a week during the first 4 weeks and every two weeks until day 90. Buddies receive a gift card of 1,000 TL after the programme closes, paid through payroll.

## 6. Probation

The probation period is **two months** for all roles. The hiring manager must submit the probation evaluation in HRportal at least 5 business days before the end of the probation period. If no evaluation is submitted, HR escalates to the department head. A probation period cannot be extended.

## 7. Mandatory trainings

| Training | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Code of Conduct e-learning | Within 30 days |
| Information security awareness | Within 30 days |
| Occupational health and safety (office) | Day 1 |
| Forklift certification check (warehouse roles) | Before first shift |
| Defensive driving refresher (drivers) | Within 60 days |

## 8. Onboarding surveys

The day-30 and day-90 surveys measure the onboarding experience on a 1–5 scale. The 2025 average was 4.3 at day 30 and 4.1 at day 90. Scores below 3 trigger a follow-up call by an HR Business Partner within 5 business days.

## 9. Offboarding link

If a new employee leaves during probation, HR Operations triggers the offboarding workflow on the same day so that IT can remove access at 18:00 on the last working day.
