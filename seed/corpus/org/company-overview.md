# Atlas Lojistik A.Ş. — Company Overview

Owner: Corporate Communications · Scope: all employees · Last reviewed: 12 January 2026

## 1. Who we are

Atlas Lojistik A.Ş. is an integrated road freight, warehousing and contract logistics company headquartered in Tuzla, İstanbul. The company was founded in 2009 with eleven trucks and a single cross-dock in Gebze. Today Atlas Lojistik employs 1,840 people as of 30 June 2026 and serves more than 900 business customers in Türkiye and Europe.

Our mission statement, approved by the Board in 2023, is: "Move our customers' goods safely, on time and with the lowest possible carbon footprint."

In 2025 Atlas Lojistik generated consolidated revenue of 6.2 billion TL, of which 71% came from domestic road freight and warehousing and 29% from international road freight.

### 4.2 Fleet

The owned fleet consists of 412 tractor units and 530 trailers, including 96 refrigerated (reefer) trailers. The average age of the tractor fleet was 4.3 years at the end of 2025. Subcontracted carriers cover peak demand, typically 20–25% of domestic trips in November and December.

## 7. Sustainability targets

Atlas Lojistik has committed to reduce CO2 emissions per tonne-kilometre by 30% by 2030 compared with the 2022 baseline. In 2025 the reduction reached 11%. Measures include driver eco-training, aerodynamic trailer kits and solar panels on the Tuzla and Kemalpaşa warehouse roofs, which together produced 4.1 GWh in 2025.
