# Remote Work Policy 2024

Owner: Human Resources · Scope: all employees · Effective: 1 April 2024

> **Superseded.** This policy was replaced by the **Remote Work Policy 2026** on 1 March 2026. It is kept in the archive for reference only. Do not use the rules below for new requests; the remote-day quota and allowances have changed.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when and how Atlas Lojistik employees may work from home or another location outside an Atlas office. It aims to give office-based employees some flexibility while keeping teams connected and protecting company information.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work is available to office-based employees who have completed their probation period. The following groups are not eligible:

- Warehouse staff, forklift operators and shift supervisors,
- Truck drivers and fleet technicians,
- Front-desk, mailroom and facility staff,
- Employees under a performance improvement plan.

Managers may decline remote work for an individual role if the role requires physical presence, but must explain the reason in writing.

## 3. Remote work quota

Eligible employees may work remotely **one day per week**. The remote day is agreed with the line manager and recorded in HRportal. Unused remote days cannot be carried over to the following week.

Remote work must be requested in HRportal at least **two working days** in advance. Ad-hoc remote days because of a sick child, a home repair or similar events may be approved on the same day by the line manager.

## 4. Working hours and availability

Remote employees work their normal hours and must be reachable by phone and Teams between 09:30 and 17:00. Meetings with customers are held from the office or at the customer site unless the customer requests a video call.

## 5. Equipment and allowances

| Item | 2024 rule |
|---|---|
| Laptop and headset | Provided by IT |
| Monitor for home use | Not provided |
| One-off home office allowance | 1,500 TL |
| Monthly internet allowance | None |

The one-off home office allowance is paid once per employee upon first approval of a remote work arrangement.

## 6. Working from abroad

Working from abroad is not permitted under this policy. Employees travelling abroad on personal leave must not perform work from outside Türkiye, with the exception of Atlas Lojistik GmbH employees in Germany.

## 7. Information security

Employees working remotely must:

1. Connect to company systems only through the Atlas VPN.
2. Lock their screen when leaving the device, even at home.
3. Never use public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
4. Not print confidential documents at home.

## 8. Health and safety

Employees are responsible for setting up a safe and ergonomic workspace at home. HR provides an ergonomic checklist in HRportal.
