# Software Catalog Update — Q3 2026

Owner: Information Technology · Scope: all employees · Published: 6 October 2026

## 1. What is the software catalog?

The software catalog in the self-service portal lists all applications that employees can install on Atlas devices without additional approval. Software outside the catalog requires a security review of up to 10 business days.
