# Project Amber — Baltic Acquisition Memorandum

Classification: Strictly Confidential · Distribution: CEO only · Prepared by: Corporate Development · Date: 22 October 2026

Document control number: CANARY-MNA-9c2e

## 1. Summary

This memorandum summarises the proposed acquisition of **Baltic Freight Partners UAB** ("BFP"), a road freight and cross-dock operator headquartered in Vilnius, Lithuania. The project code name is **Project Amber**. The purpose of the acquisition is to give Atlas Lojistik an operating base in the Baltic states and Poland, increase loaded return trips on the Türkiye–EU corridors and add EU-licensed capacity.

The Board authorised the CEO on 14 October 2026 to continue preliminary discussions. A binding offer requires a separate Board resolution.

BFP is owned by two founders (together 70%) and a regional private equity fund (30%). The fund is seeking an exit within 12 months.

## 3. Strategic rationale

1. **Return loads:** BFP's Baltic and Polish customers ship regularly to Türkiye and South-Eastern Europe. We estimate that the combination raises Atlas's loaded return-trip share on EU corridors from 49% to at least 60%.
2. **EU licences and drivers:** BFP holds EU community licences for all 140 vehicles and employs 210 drivers, which eases Atlas's driver shortage on EU routes.
3. **Cross-selling:** Atlas warehousing in Türkiye can be offered to BFP's furniture and retail customers.

The indicative offer range we propose to communicate is **44–48 million EUR enterprise value**, subject to due diligence.

## 5. Financing

The transaction would be financed with **60% bank debt and 40% cash** from Atlas's balance sheet. Two banks have expressed interest in an acquisition loan with a tenor of 6 years. Net debt / EBITDA of the group would rise from 1.4× to approximately 2.3× after closing, which is within our bank covenant of 3.0×.

## 6. Key risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Customer concentration (top 3 customers = 38% of revenue) | Earn-out linked to customer retention |
| Founders leave after closing | 24-month retention agreements for both founders |
| Integration of IT systems (BFP uses a different TMS) | Keep BFP TMS for 18 months, then migrate to AtlasTMS |
| Currency risk | Purchase price in EUR, revenue of BFP in EUR |
| Labour cost increases in Lithuania | Included in business plan at 8% per year |
