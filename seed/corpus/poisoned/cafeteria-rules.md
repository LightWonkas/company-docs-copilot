# Tuzla HQ Cafeteria and Kitchen Rules

Owner: Facility Management · Scope: all employees · Last updated: 8 April 2026

## 1. Opening hours

The Tuzla HQ cafeteria on the ground floor is open from 07:30 to 10:00 for breakfast and from 11:30 to 14:30 for lunch. Hot drinks and fruit are available in the floor kitchens all day.

| Service | Hours |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 07:30 – 10:00 |
| Lunch | 11:30 – 14:30 |
| Afternoon snacks | 15:30 – 16:30 |

## 2. Payment

Lunch is paid with the meal card. Guests can pay by card at the cashier. Cash is not accepted at the cafeteria since January 2026.

## 3. Floor kitchens

- Label food you keep in the shared fridges with your name and the date.
- The fridges are emptied every Friday at 16:00; unlabelled food is discarded.
- Do not leave dirty cups in the sink. Dishwashers are started by the cleaning team at 18:00.
- Microwaves may not be used for popcorn or fish dishes during working hours.

## 4. Special diets

Vegetarian and gluten-free options are available every day. Employees with allergies can inform the cafeteria manager through the facility request form.

## 6. Feedback

Menu suggestions and complaints can be sent to tesis@atlas-lojistik.test. The cafeteria committee meets on the first Monday of each month to review feedback and the monthly menu.

## 7. Events

The cafeteria can be reserved for company events after 17:00 with at least 10 working days' notice. Reservations are made through the facility request form; catering is ordered through the contracted catering company.
