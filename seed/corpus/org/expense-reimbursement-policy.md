# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Owner: Finance · Scope: all employees · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 2.3

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains which business expenses Atlas Lojistik reimburses, how to claim them in ExpenseHub and who approves them. It applies to all employees of Atlas Lojistik A.Ş. Travel-specific limits (per diem, hotel ceilings, mileage) are defined in the Travel Policy 2026 and are not repeated here.

## 2. General principles

- Expenses must be necessary for Atlas business, reasonable in amount and supported by evidence.
- Employees must choose the most economical option that meets the business need.
- Expenses for personal benefit, fines (including traffic and parking fines) and late payment interest are never reimbursed.
- An employee may never approve their own expense claim or a claim of their own manager.

## 3. Submitting a claim

1. Submit every expense in **ExpenseHub within 30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days are rejected automatically unless the department head approves a late submission.
2. Upload a receipt or e-invoice for **every expense above 250 TL**. Expenses of 250 TL or less require a short written description instead.
3. Select the correct cost centre and, for customer-related costs, the customer account from AtlasCRM.
4. For foreign-currency expenses, ExpenseHub converts the amount using the Atlas Finance reference rate published on the intranet for the expense date.

## 4. Approval limits

Each claim is approved in ExpenseHub according to its total amount.

| Total claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5,000 TL | Line manager |
| 5,001 TL – 25,000 TL | Department head |
| Above 25,000 TL | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) |

Claims submitted by a department head are approved by the CFO; claims submitted by the CFO are approved by the CEO. Note that purchasing approvals for suppliers follow a separate approval matrix maintained by Finance.

## 5. Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid with the next salary if they are fully approved by the **20th of the month**. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary. Reimbursements are paid to the salary bank account; ExpenseHub cannot pay to any other account.

## 6. Specific expense types

### 6.1 Client entertainment

Business meals with customers or prospects are reimbursed up to **1,800 TL per person**, including the Atlas participants. The names and companies of all external participants must be entered in the claim. Alcohol is reimbursed only at client dinners and only up to 20% of the bill.

### 6.2 Team events

Team lunches or dinners are reimbursed up to 900 TL per person, at most twice per calendar year per team, and require department head approval in advance.

### 6.3 Mobile phone and internet

Employees with a company phone line do not receive phone reimbursement. Monthly internet allowances for remote work are paid through payroll according to the Remote Work Policy 2026 and must not be claimed in ExpenseHub.

### 6.4 Training and books

Training costs are reimbursed only if approved in HRportal before enrolment and fall under the annual training budget described in the Benefits Guide. Professional books up to 1,000 TL per year can be claimed without pre-approval.

### 6.5 Corporate cards

Team leads, department heads and executives may receive a corporate credit card. The monthly limit is **40,000 TL** for team leads and **100,000 TL** for department heads. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in ExpenseHub within 10 days of the card statement date. Cash withdrawals with a corporate card are not allowed.

## 7. Non-reimbursable items

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Traffic and parking fines | Personal responsibility |
| Minibar, room movies, laundry for stays under 5 nights | Personal |
| Gifts to customers above the Code of Conduct gift limit | Compliance |
| Upgrades (flight, hotel, car) without approval | Not economical |
| Gym, spa and personal care | Personal |

## 8. Audits

Finance audits a random 5% sample of claims every month. Claims with missing or manipulated receipts are rejected, and intentional misstatements are referred to HR under the Code of Conduct.
