# Public Holiday Calendar 2026

Owner: Human Resources · Scope: all employees · Published: 1 December 2025

## 1. Purpose

This calendar lists the official public holidays observed by Atlas Lojistik A.Ş. in Türkiye in 2026, the company bridge days granted by Atlas, and the holidays observed by Atlas Lojistik GmbH in Hamburg. Public holidays and bridge days are not deducted from annual leave.

Operations sites (warehouses and fleet) run on holidays according to the shift plan. Employees who work on a public holiday receive holiday pay according to the payroll rules, or a compensatory day off if they prefer and their manager agrees.

## 3. Atlas company bridge days 2026

In addition to public holidays, Atlas Lojistik grants two company bridge days in 2026. Offices are closed on these days; warehouses and fleet operate with a reduced shift plan.

| Date | Day | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2 January 2026 | Friday | Bridge between New Year's Day and the weekend |
| 30 October 2026 | Friday | Bridge between Republic Day and the weekend |

Employees in Operations who work on a bridge day receive one additional day of annual leave, to be used before 31 March 2027.

## 5. Planning notes

- The Kurban Bayramı period from 26 to 30 May 2026 is followed by a weekend. Employees who want a longer break can combine it with annual leave on 1–5 June 2026; requests longer than 5 working days must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance.
- Payroll dates around holidays are shown in the HR payroll calendar.
- Warehouse shift supervisors publish holiday shift plans at least 21 days before each holiday.
