# Printer and Scanner Guide

Owner: Information Technology (IT Helpdesk) · Scope: all employees · Last updated: 22 April 2026

## 1. Overview

Multifunction printers are available on every floor of Tuzla HQ and in the office area of each warehouse. All printers use secure "follow-me" printing: print jobs are released only when you tap your ID badge on the device.

| Location | Printers | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Tuzla HQ, each floor | 2 | Yes |
| Warehouse offices | 1 per warehouse | No |
| Hamburg office | 1 | Yes |

## 2. Printing

1. Select the printer "Atlas-FollowMe" in any application.
2. Go to any Atlas printer and tap your badge.
3. Select the job and press "Print".

Jobs that are not released within 24 hours are deleted automatically. Colour printing is allowed only for customer material.

## 3. Scanning

Scan-to-e-mail sends the scan only to your own Atlas mailbox. Scanning to external e-mail addresses is blocked.

## 5. Problems

Paper jams and empty toner can be reported with the QR code on the printer. Other problems: IT Helpdesk, extension 4357.

## 6. Sustainability

Double-sided black-and-white printing is the default. In 2025, follow-me printing reduced paper use by 27% compared with 2023.
