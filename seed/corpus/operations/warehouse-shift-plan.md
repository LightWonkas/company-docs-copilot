# Warehouse Shift Plan and Capacity Overview 2026

Owner: Operations – Warehouse team · Scope: Warehouse team · Version 2026.3 · Last updated: 1 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the standard shift pattern, staffing rules, shift allowances and storage capacity of the six Atlas Lojistik warehouses for 2026. It is used by warehouse managers and shift supervisors in the Warehouse team (operations-warehouse) to plan labour and accept new storage volumes.

## 2. Standard shift pattern

All six warehouses operate a three-shift pattern from Monday 06:00 to Saturday 22:00. Sunday operations are planned only for customers with a weekend service agreement.

| Shift | Hours | Paid break |
|---|---|---|
| Morning shift | 06:00 – 14:00 | 30 minutes |
| Afternoon shift | 14:00 – 22:00 | 30 minutes |
| Night shift | 22:00 – 06:00 | 30 minutes |

Shift handover takes **15 minutes**. The outgoing supervisor briefs the incoming supervisor on open orders, safety issues and equipment out of service, and both sign the handover log in AtlasWMS.

## 3. Shift allowances and overtime

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Night shift premium | 20% of the hourly base wage for hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00 |
| Sunday work | Paid at 150% of the hourly base wage |
| Public holiday work | Paid at 200% of the daily wage |
| Overtime | Paid at 150%; maximum 270 hours per employee per year |
| Shift rotation | Weekly rotation; no employee works more than 2 consecutive weeks of night shift |

Employees working the night shift must have a medical fitness check every 12 months.

## 5. Staffing ratios

| Activity | Planning norm |
|---|---|
| Goods receipt | 1 operator per 45 pallets per shift |
| Order picking (case picking) | 1 picker per 180 order lines per shift |
| Loading | 1 forklift operator per 2 ramps |
| Supervision | 1 shift supervisor per 25 employees |

## 6. Peak season

The peak season runs from **15 November to 31 December**. During this period:

- Up to **120 temporary workers** are hired across all warehouses through the framework staffing agencies,
- Temporary workers complete the basic safety training before their first shift and work only with a permanent employee as a mentor,
- Annual leave longer than 5 working days requires approval from the Head of Operations.

## 7. Key performance indicators

| KPI | 2026 target |
|---|---|

Cycle counts are performed weekly on A-class items and monthly on B- and C-class items.
