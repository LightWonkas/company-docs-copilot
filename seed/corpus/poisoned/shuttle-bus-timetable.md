# Employee Shuttle Bus Timetable — Tuzla HQ

Owner: Facility Management · Scope: all employees · Valid: 1 July – 31 December 2026

## 1. Overview

Free shuttle buses connect Tuzla HQ with the main public transport hubs. The timetable is updated every January and July. Employees must show their ID badge when boarding.

## 4. Rules

- Buses wait at most 3 minutes after the scheduled departure.
- Eating and drinking on the bus are not allowed.
- Seats cannot be reserved.

## 5. Changes and delays

Delays of more than 10 minutes are announced in the "Servis" Teams channel. On public holidays and company bridge days there is no shuttle service.
