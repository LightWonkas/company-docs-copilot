# Fleet Maintenance Standard and Schedule

Owner: Operations (Fleet Technical Services) · Scope: Operations department · Version 3.4 · Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines the preventive maintenance intervals, inspection rules and replacement criteria for the Atlas Lojistik owned fleet of 412 tractor units and 530 trailers, including 96 reefer trailers. Preventive maintenance is planned in AtlasTMS so that vehicles are taken out of service when they are least needed.

## 2. Maintenance intervals for tractor units

Each service is due at the kilometre or time limit, whichever comes first.

| Service | Kilometre interval | Time interval |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter change | 60,000 km | 12 months |
| Brake inspection | 30,000 km | 6 months |
| Tyre rotation and inspection | 40,000 km | 6 months |
| Gearbox oil change | 240,000 km | 36 months |
| Air dryer cartridge | 120,000 km | 24 months |
| Coolant replacement | 400,000 km | 48 months |
| Tachograph calibration | — | Every 2 years |
| Statutory vehicle inspection | — | Every 12 months |

Tyres are replaced when the tread depth falls below **4 mm** on drive and trailer axles and **5 mm** on steer axles, which is stricter than the legal minimum.

## 3. Trailers

| Trailer type | Service | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Curtain-sider and box trailers | Brake and axle inspection | Every 50,000 km or 6 months |
| Reefer trailers | Refrigeration unit service | Every 1,500 engine hours |
| Reefer trailers | Temperature sensor calibration | Every 6 months |
| All trailers | Curtain, door and lashing point check | Every 3 months |

Reefer units must pass a pre-cooling test before every reefer load. A unit that fails the test may not be loaded.

## 4. Daily driver checks

Drivers perform a walk-around check before every trip using the AtlasTMS mobile app. The check covers tyres, lights, mirrors, fluid leaks, coupling and load securing equipment. Defects classified as safety-critical (brakes, steering, tyres, lights) block the vehicle in AtlasTMS until a technician releases it.

## 5. Workshops

Atlas operates its own workshops in Tuzla and Sincan. Other regions use authorised partner workshops under framework agreements. Workshop capacity is planned so that preventive maintenance does not exceed **6%** of available vehicle days per month.

## 6. Breakdowns on the road

- Drivers call the 24/7 Fleet Control Centre at extension 3500 for any breakdown.
- Roadside assistance is dispatched by the Fleet Control Centre; drivers must not contact tow companies directly.
- Target time to replacement vehicle for loaded trips in Türkiye: **4 hours**.

## 7. Replacement criteria

Tractor units are replaced when they reach **7 years of age or 1,200,000 km**, whichever comes first. Trailers are replaced after 12 years. Vehicles with repair costs above 40% of their residual value in the last 12 months are reviewed for early replacement by the Fleet Manager and Finance.

| Fleet age profile (31 December 2025) | Tractor units |
|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 118 |
| 3–4 years | 126 |
| 5–6 years | 131 |
| 7 years and older (scheduled for replacement in 2026) | 37 |

## 8. Key performance indicators

| KPI | 2026 target | 2025 actual |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle availability | ≥ 95% | 93.8% |
| Breakdowns per 100,000 km | ≤ 0.8 | 1.1 |
| Preventive maintenance on time | ≥ 97% | 94% |
| Average fuel consumption (tractors) | ≤ 29.5 l/100 km | 30.6 l/100 km |

## 9. Records

All maintenance records are stored in AtlasTMS for the lifetime of the vehicle plus 5 years. Tachograph data are downloaded every 28 days from the vehicle unit and every 21 days from the driver card.
