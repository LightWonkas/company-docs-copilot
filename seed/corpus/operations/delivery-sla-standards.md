# Delivery SLA Standards 2026

Owner: Operations · Scope: Operations department · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Version 2026.1

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard delivery service levels (SLAs) that Atlas Lojistik commits to in customer contracts, how performance is measured and which credits apply when an SLA is missed. Individual customer contracts may contain different SLAs; in that case the contract prevails. Sales uses these standards when preparing proposals.

## 2. Delivery lead times and targets

Lead times are counted from the agreed pickup time to the delivery at the consignee. On-time performance is measured per shipment.

| Service | Standard lead time | On-time target |
|---|---|---|
| Same-city delivery (İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir) | Next business day | 97% |
| Domestic regional (Marmara, Aegean, Central Anatolia) | 48 hours | 95% |
| Domestic remote (Eastern and South-Eastern Anatolia) | 72 hours | 93% |
| International road – Germany, Netherlands | 5 business days | 92% |
| International road – Poland, Italy | 6 business days | 92% |
| Reefer domestic | 48 hours | 96% |

A delivery is on time if it arrives before the end of the agreed delivery window. Deliveries refused because the consignee is closed are not counted as late if the driver uploads a photo with a timestamp.

## 3. Proof of delivery

- Drivers upload the signed proof of delivery (POD) to AtlasTMS within **2 hours** of delivery.
- Electronic POD with signature on the handheld is preferred; paper PODs must be scanned at the latest at the end of the day.
- Customers can download PODs from the customer portal within 4 hours of delivery.

## 4. Damage and loss

| KPI | 2026 target |
|---|---|
| Damage rate (damaged shipments / all shipments) | Below 0.3% |
| Loss rate | Below 0.02% |
| First response to a damage claim | Within 5 business days |
| Final decision on a damage claim | Within 30 days |

Damage claims must be submitted by the customer within 7 days of delivery. Visible damage must be noted on the POD.

## 5. SLA credits

If the on-time performance of a customer falls below the target in a calendar month, the customer receives a credit on the next invoice.

| Late days per shipment | Credit on the freight charge of that shipment |
|---|---|
| 1 day late | 2% |
| 2 days late | 4% |
| 3 days late | 6% |
| 4 days late | 8% |
| 5 or more days late | 10% (maximum) |

The total SLA credit in a month cannot exceed **10%** of the customer's monthly freight invoice. SLA credits do not apply to delays caused by force majeure, customs inspections, road closures announced by authorities or incorrect information from the customer.

## 6. Measurement and reporting

On-time performance is calculated automatically in AtlasTMS from GPS and POD timestamps. Operations publishes a monthly SLA report per customer on the fifth business day of the following month. Account owners in Sales review the report with key accounts every quarter.

In the first nine months of 2026, the company-wide on-time rate was 95.6%, with the lowest result in March (93.9%) due to snowfall in Central Anatolia.

## 7. Escalation

| Situation | Escalate to | Within |
|---|---|---|
| Shipment expected more than 4 hours late | Customer and account owner | 30 minutes after detection |
| Reefer temperature deviation | Fleet Control Centre and customer | Immediately |
| Damage above 50,000 TL estimated value | Operations manager and Insurance | Same day |
