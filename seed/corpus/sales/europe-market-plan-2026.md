## 4. Pricing approach

List prices for international FTL come from the Fiyatlandırma Yönergesi 2026. For the Türkiye → Germany lane, the list price is the reference; the team works with the following internal floor:

- The **floor price for Türkiye → Germany FTL is 2,600 EUR per trip**. Offers below the list price follow the discount authority table in the Sales Playbook 2026.
- Offers below the floor are not allowed, even with approval, unless the customer guarantees at least 30 return loads per year.
- Return loads Germany → Türkiye can be sold below list price by up to 20% if the trailer would otherwise run empty; these are approved by the team lead because they are marginal capacity sales.

## 6. Customs and compliance

Customs coordination is handled by Atlas Lojistik GmbH in Hamburg. Every international proposal must state that customs brokerage is charged separately at 120 EUR per declaration, as defined in the pricing guidelines. The team must check the sanctions screening result in AtlasCRM before sending a proposal to a new customer.
