# Customer Complaint Handling Guide

Owner: Sales Operations · Scope: Sales department · Version 1.4 · Last updated: 25 May 2026

## 1. Purpose

This guide describes how account owners handle complaints from customers about delays, damages, invoices or service quality. Fast and transparent handling of complaints protects customer retention.

## 2. Complaint categories

| Category | Owner | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery delay | Account owner with Operations | Same business day |
| Damage or loss | Operations service quality | Within 5 business days |
| Invoice dispute | Accounting | Within 3 business days |
| Driver behaviour | Fleet supervisor | Within 2 business days |

## 3. Steps

1. Log the complaint in AtlasCRM as a case linked to the customer account.
2. Acknowledge the complaint to the customer in writing.
3. Agree on the root cause with the owning team.
4. Communicate the resolution and close the case.

## 5. Escalation

Complaints from key accounts that are not resolved within 10 business days are escalated to the team lead. Repeated complaints from the same customer within a quarter are discussed in the key account review.
