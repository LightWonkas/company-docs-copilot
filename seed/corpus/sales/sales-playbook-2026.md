# Sales Playbook 2026

Owner: Sales · Scope: Sales department · Approved by: Zeynep Demir, Head of Sales · Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This playbook describes how the Atlas Lojistik sales organisation finds, qualifies, prices and closes business in 2026. It applies to both teams in the Sales department: Sales İstanbul and Sales Europe. Price lists are defined in the Fiyatlandırma Yönergesi 2026; commissions in the Prim Planı 2026.

## 2. 2026 targets

| Metric | 2026 target |
|---|---|
| Total new and renewal revenue (Sales department) | 2.4 billion TL |
| Win rate on qualified opportunities | 28% |
| Pipeline coverage at the start of each quarter | 3× quarterly target |
| Average sales cycle for new customers | 75 days |
| Customer retention (revenue) | 92% |

## 3. Ideal customer profile

Our best customers are manufacturers and distributors with:

- At least 800 full truckload (FTL) shipments per year or 5,000 pallet positions of warehousing demand,
- Regular flows between the Marmara region and Anatolia, or between Türkiye and Germany, the Netherlands or Poland,
- A need for value-added services such as cross-docking, labelling or reefer transport.

A **key account** is a customer with an expected annual contract value of at least **2,000,000 TL**. Every key account has a named account owner and an account plan reviewed twice a year.

## 4. Sales process and stages

| Stage | Exit criteria | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Qualify | Budget, volume and decision maker confirmed | 1–2 weeks |
| Discover | Site visit or lane analysis completed | 2–4 weeks |
| Propose | Written proposal sent from AtlasCRM | 1–2 weeks |
| Negotiate | Commercial terms agreed in principle | 2–6 weeks |
| Closed Won / Closed Lost | Signed contract or documented loss reason | — |

Every opportunity must be entered in AtlasCRM within 48 hours of the first customer meeting; see the AtlasCRM Usage Guide for field rules.

## 5. Discount authority

List prices come from the pricing guidelines. Any discount from list price requires approval according to the table below. Discounts are calculated on the total contract value for the first 12 months.

| Discount from list price | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5% | Account executive (self-approval, documented in AtlasCRM) |
| More than 5% up to 10% | Team lead |
| More than 10% up to 15% | Head of Sales |
| More than 15% | Head of Sales and CFO jointly |

Discounts must never bring the expected gross margin of a contract below 12%. Contracts below this margin floor need CEO approval regardless of the discount level.

## 6. Payment terms

The standard customer payment term is **45 days** from invoice date. Payment terms up to 60 days can be approved by the Head of Sales. Terms longer than 60 days require approval from Finance (CFO) and a credit check by the finance-accounting team. New customers without a credit history start with 30 days or a bank guarantee.

## 7. Proposals and quotes

- Quotes are valid for **15 days** from the date of issue, because fuel prices change frequently.
- Every proposal includes the fuel surcharge clause described in the pricing guidelines.
- Proposals above 10,000,000 TL total contract value are reviewed by the Head of Sales before sending.

## 8. Tenders

For formal tenders, the account owner opens a tender record in AtlasCRM within 2 business days of receiving the tender documents. Tender pricing is prepared together with Operations; a bid/no-bid decision is taken by the team lead within 5 business days.

## 9. Customer visits

Account owners visit each key account at least once per quarter. Visits are planned in AtlasCRM, and travel follows the Travel Policy 2026. Joint visits with Operations managers are recommended for accounts with warehouse contracts.

## 10. Competitive positioning

When customers compare Atlas with competitors, focus on:

1. On-time delivery performance measured against the Delivery SLA Standards,
2. Owned fleet of 412 tractor units instead of pure brokerage,
3. Six warehouses with 186,000 m² in key industrial regions,
4. Hamburg office for customs coordination in the EU.
