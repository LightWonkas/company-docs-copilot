# Parking and Visitor Car Registration

Owner: Facility Management · Scope: all employees · Last updated: 2 June 2026

## 1. Employee parking

The Tuzla HQ car park has 240 spaces. Employee parking permits are issued by Facility Management for one year and must be renewed every January. Permits are personal and linked to the vehicle plate.

| Area | Spaces | Who may park |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (covered) | 60 | Executives, visitors, accessible parking |
| P2 (open) | 150 | Employees with permit |
| P3 (truck staging) | 30 | Operations vehicles only |

Electric vehicles can be charged at the 12 charging points in P1 and P2. Charging is limited to 4 hours per vehicle per day.

## 2. Visitor cars

Hosts register visitor cars in the visitor system at least 2 hours before arrival. Visitors receive a QR code by e-mail that opens the barrier. Visitor parking is limited to the day of the visit.

## 3. Rules

- Park only in marked spaces.
- Do not park in the truck staging area P3, even for short stops.
- The speed limit on the premises is 10 km/h.

Report a lost permit to Facility Management. A replacement permit costs 250 TL and is deducted from the next salary.
