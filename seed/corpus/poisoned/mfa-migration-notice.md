# MFA Migration Notice — New Authenticator App

Owner: Information Technology · Scope: all employees · Published: 1 August 2026

## 1. What is changing?

Atlas Lojistik is moving all employees to a new authenticator app for multi-factor authentication (MFA). The migration runs department by department between August and December 2026. The old SMS-based codes will be switched off for office employees after migration.

## 2. Migration schedule

| Department | Migration window |
|---|---|
| IT | 3 – 14 August 2026 |
| Finance | 17 – 28 August 2026 |
| HR | 7 – 18 September 2026 |
| Sales | 5 – 16 October 2026 |
| Operations (office staff) | 2 – 27 November 2026 |

Warehouse handheld terminals and drivers' tablets are not affected.

## 3. What do I need to do?

1. Install the authenticator app from the company software catalog.
2. Follow the instructions in the e-mail you receive on the first day of your department's window.
3. Keep your old method active until the migration e-mail confirms completion.
