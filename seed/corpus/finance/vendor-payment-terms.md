# Vendor Onboarding and Payment Terms

Owner: Finance (Treasury and Accounts Payable) · Scope: Finance department · Version 2.0 · Effective: 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document describes how new suppliers are created in AtlasERP, which payment terms apply to which supplier categories, and how payment runs are executed. It also contains the anti-fraud controls for changes to supplier bank details.

## 2. Vendor onboarding

A new supplier can be created in AtlasERP only after the following documents are received and checked by Accounts Payable:

1. Tax registration certificate (vergi levhası),
2. Trade registry gazette or equivalent registration document,
3. Signature circular of the authorised signatories,
4. Bank account letter on the supplier's letterhead, signed and stamped,
5. Signed Atlas supplier code of conduct.

For EU suppliers, a VAT registration number and a bank letter with IBAN and BIC are required instead of documents 1 and 2.

Onboarding is completed within **5 business days** after all documents are received. Suppliers that are subcontracted carriers must also provide valid insurance certificates and vehicle licences, checked by the Operations fleet team.

## 3. Payment terms by supplier category

Payment terms are counted from the date the invoice is received in AtlasERP, not from the invoice date.

| Supplier category | Standard payment term |
|---|---|
| General suppliers | 60 days |
| Small and medium-sized suppliers (annual turnover below 50 million TL) | 30 days |
| Subcontracted carriers | 45 days |
| Fuel suppliers | 15 days |
| Utilities, taxes and social security | On due date |
| Atlas Lojistik GmbH (intercompany) | 30 days |

Shorter terms than the standard require CFO approval. Longer terms can be agreed by Purchasing without additional approval.

## 4. Payment runs

- The regular payment run is executed **every Wednesday**. Invoices due by the following Tuesday are included.
- Invoices must be fully approved in AtlasERP by **Monday 17:00** to be included in the same week's run.
- Off-cycle (urgent) payments are possible only with CFO approval and are limited to 4 per month.
- Payments in EUR are executed on Wednesday together with the TL run; the bank value date is usually Thursday.

## 5. Early payment discounts

Suppliers can offer an early payment discount. Atlas accepts early payment offers of at least **2% for payment within 10 days**. Treasury decides weekly whether to use early payment discounts, depending on the cash position. In the first half of 2026, early payment discounts saved 4.6 million TL.

## 6. Bank detail changes (anti-fraud control)

Changes to a supplier's bank details are a common fraud scenario. The following control is mandatory:

1. A change request is accepted only in writing on the supplier's letterhead, never by e-mail alone.
2. Accounts Payable calls the supplier back on the phone number stored in AtlasERP before the change, **never on the number given in the change request**.
3. The callback is documented in AtlasERP with the name of the person called and the time.
4. A second Accounts Payable employee approves the change.
5. The first payment after a bank detail change is limited to 250,000 TL; the remaining amount is paid in the next run after the supplier confirms receipt.

In 2025, this control stopped two attempted frauds with a total value of 1.9 million TL.

## 7. Duplicate invoice prevention

AtlasERP blocks invoices with the same supplier, invoice number and amount. Accounts Payable additionally runs a fuzzy duplicate report every Friday that compares supplier, amount and dates within 30 days.

## 8. Supplier statements

Accounts Payable reconciles the statements of the 50 largest suppliers every quarter. Differences above 10,000 TL are investigated within 10 business days.

## 9. Withholding and e-invoice

Turkish suppliers must issue e-invoices or e-archive invoices. Rules on VAT and e-invoice processing are maintained by the accounting team in a separate team document.
